Subject: Multi-volume folder access — Transfer Hub / Wake & Sync / related apps (Volume 3)

Dear UGREEN NAS Developer Platform Team,

Some users reported that they could not access folders on **Volume 3** (and other storage pools beyond the first granted path) in our apps. We investigated and rebuilt the App Center folder grants so users can select folders on any volume.

## What we changed

Previously, some packages relied on a multi-select path parameter. That approach was unreliable in practice for additional volumes. We now use up to **three separate single-folder grants**:

1. UGOS folder (required)
2. Extra UGOS folder 2 (optional)
3. Extra UGOS folder 3 (optional)

Users may leave the optional fields empty. Folders may sit on Volume 1, Volume 2, Volume 3, or any other pool. After changing grants, the app should be restarted.

We also fixed the first-password setup screen for Transfer Hub and Wake & Sync (New + Confirm always shown when setting the initial app password).

Related updates in this iteration:
- Stats Hub lists only real mounted volumes (no empty stubs)
- Moving Files 0.2.0.0055: activation hang related to an extra-folder parameter removed; volume roots limited to real mountpoints
- Backup Verifier / Lock & Key: multi-volume mount and discovery improvements
